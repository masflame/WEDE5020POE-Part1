Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a cake?

We recommend ordering celebration cakes at least three days in advance.

Yes. Tell us about your theme, colours, and number of servings in your enquiry.

Do you cater for allergies or dietary needs?

Please include your dietary requirements in your message so we can confirm the available options.

Can I place a bulk order for an event?

Yes. We can prepare bulk orders for meetings, celebrations, and special events.

When can I collect my order?

Collection times are confirmed with you once we have received and accepted your enquiry.
